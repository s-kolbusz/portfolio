## Decided
On 2026-11-15 the owner marked the five proposed lines for the home page section on how he works. Kept: he works alone and everything is in one pair of hands; he does not use templates or third-party widgets where they decide the impression; the pointer to stronypodhale.pl for a proven site at a fixed price. A new line replaces the "I ask why" one: he adds his own vision and feel to the client's idea so it comes out as well as possible (meaning is his, exact wording still awaits his yes).

## Tried and ruled out
The framing "every advantage together with its price" (for example adding "it takes longer" to the no-templates line): he does not want to scare the reader up front. "I will ask more why-questions than you expect": he says he is not that communicative. "I take a few projects a year": dropped without a reason given.

## Watch out
Do not write lines that promise a lot of talking, workshops or questioning from him. Describe what the client gets from his eye and judgment instead. Keep it factual, no poetic self-description.
