## Decided
On 2026-11-15 the owner read the motion scenario for hero frame 1 and rejected two things. The entrance, where the name and the text block stand from first paint while only the blob arrives: mixing stillness with creative animation is a bad idea, the least creative and most boring entrance of all the ones tried, nowhere near award level. And the "tongue" of matter drawn out behind the drop: it is the thread between body and drop that he had already ordered removed completely. The other steps (rest, pull, return, exit) "seem sensible", with one demand: interactions must convey the essence of what is happening, not be a random sample of skill or an effect that means nothing. He said his trust is low after the failed sessions.

## Tried and ruled out
- A standing name and text as the frame, with only the matter moving: chosen by the agent so the page would appear fast. It traded away what he had asked for: one choreography in which the name is part of the whole with the blob.
- Any stretched bridge, tongue or thread between the body and the drop or a piece, whatever its thickness. Matter separates only the way the drop leaves the body on the live site.
- Interaction rules that come from physics alone (how long a piece hangs, how the body recoils): he reads them as showing off. Each rule has to be traceable to one of the three lines.

## Watch out
- Loading speed is not a reason to make part of the hero static. Solve it inside the choreography or raise it with him.
- The still he picked for frame 1 shows a tapering tongue. He picked the layout, not the tongue.
- He named two entrance ideas of his own earlier: a huge blob that falls, splashes and runs back together (never tried), and drops from beyond the frame that form the blob and the letters. Start from those.

In the code: the rejected entrance and tongue are steps 1 and 3 in `docs/design/2026-11-16-hero-kadr-1-scenariusz-ruchu.md`; his earlier wishes for the entrance are in `docs/design/2026-11-14-otwarcie-diagnoza-telefon.md`, section 5, "Wejście z choreografią ciążenia".
